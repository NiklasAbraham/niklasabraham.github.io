I am an MSc student in Machine Learning at the University of Tübingen, and from October 2026 a Recognised Student in the Department of Statistics at the University of Oxford. There I am writing my master's thesis with the [Oxford Protein Informatics Group](https://www.blopig.com/) (OPIG), supervised by Dr. Nele Quast and Prof. Charlotte Deane, with Prof. Philipp Hennig in Tübingen as formal supervisor. I am a scholarship holder of the German Scholarship Foundation ([Studienstiftung des Deutschen Volkes](https://www.studienstiftung.de/)).

My thesis asks why diffusion-based co-folding models such as AlphaFold3, Boltz-2 and OpenFold3 barely sample: the denoiser returns a near-final structure at the first reverse step, and the conditional distribution collapses toward a single pose. I want to measure where that collapse sits (scaffold, interface or ligand), find out whether the missing diversity was never learned or only never sampled, and recover it without giving up physical validity. The project grows out of our [ICLR 2026 LMRL workshop paper](research/), which converted these models to deterministic probability-flow ODEs for a 20x sampling speed-up on FoldBench.

Alongside my studies I co-founded [Folaint](https://folaint.de), where I am CTO and managing director of a five-person team building a knowledge-graph foundation that LLM agents reason over.

Before that, I spent spring 2026 as a research intern at Merck KGaA's Group Science & Technology Office, working on protein function prediction for multi-site mutations. At the Institute of Biochemistry and Technical Biochemistry (University of Stuttgart) I worked with [Prof. Jürgen Pleiss](https://www.ibtb.uni-stuttgart.de/institut/team/Pleiss-00001/) on protein language models for predicting β-lactamase resistance and contributed to the [Python Enzyme Engineering Database](https://github.com/PyEED/pyeed) (PyEED). In October 2025 I won the Speed-Up Challenge at the Merck M-Boltz Hackathon with a deterministic flow-matching sampler for protein structure generation. I hold a BSc in Simulation Technology from the University of Stuttgart.

Outside research, I train for triathlon (next up: a 70.3 Ironman), coach climbing for children at KiSS e.V., and read as much non-fiction as I can find.
