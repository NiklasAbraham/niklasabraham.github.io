papers

Open source research tool

A second brain for research papers.

PaperManager turns a pile of PDFs into a knowledge graph you can read, search and talk to. Drop in a paper and it finds the metadata, writes a summary, pulls out figures and references, draws the ideas on each page as interactive visualisations, and maps how every paper in a project connects to the others.
