## B. How the money is raised (auctions, instruments)

9. **Treasury instruments**: bills 4 to 52 weeks; notes 2, 3, 5, 7, 10 years; bonds 20 and 30 years; TIPS 5, 10, 30 years; floating-rate notes 2 years, quarterly coupon indexed to the 13-week bill. TreasuryDirect, Marketable Securities. https://www.treasurydirect.gov/marketable-securities/ [primary, opened]
10. **Auction rule: single-price.** "All successful bidders get the same rate, yield, or discount margin as the highest accepted bid." Competitive bids are capped at 35 percent of the offering; noncompetitive bids at $10 million per auction and accept whatever the auction sets. https://treasurydirect.gov/auctions/how-auctions-work [primary, opened]
11. **Treasury first fills the noncompetitive bids, then competitive bids from the lowest yield upward until the offering is allotted; bids at the stop-out yield are prorated.** TreasuryDirect auction FAQs. https://www.treasurydirect.gov/help-center/faqs/auction-faqs [primary, snippet]
12. **Average interest rate on the US marketable stock: 3.518 percent on 30 Sep 2026** (bills 3.870, notes 3.383, bonds 3.466, all interest-bearing debt 3.531, TIPS 1.149, FRN 4.141). FiscalData, Average Interest Rates on Treasury Securities. https://fiscaldata.treasury.gov/datasets/average-interest-rates-treasury-securities/ [primary, opened]. Compare the 10-year yield on 15 Oct 2026, 5.10 percent (see 31): the stock was issued when rates were lower, so the average cost rises only as the old debt rolls over.
13. **Germany plans to raise about EUR 512.0 bn in 2026 through auctions** (EUR 345.5 bn capital market, EUR 176 bn Bubills); new 20-year Bund for 2026; Q3 2026 planned EUR 91 bn capital market plus EUR 47 bn money market. Deutsche Finanzagentur, issuance planning, 18 Dec 2025 and Q3 outlook 25 Jun 2026. https://www.deutsche-finanzagentur.de/en/institutional-investors/primary-market/issuance-planning [primary, snippet]
14. **UK, 8 Sep 2026: syndication of the 5 3/8% Treasury Gilt 2056, UK domestic investors about 71 percent of the allocation; gilt sales so far this financial year GBP 124.8 bn of a GBP 246.2 bn remit.** DMO press release. https://dmo.gov.uk/media/a0sirgel/pr080926.pdf [primary, snippet]
15. **Governments shorten issuance when long yields rise**: OECD report notes "many countries are rebalancing their issuance towards shorter maturities", citing higher term premia and steeper curves; UK average maturity of the debt portfolio fell from 13.74 to 13.06 years between Sep 2025 and Jun 2026 (3). [OECD snippet; DMO primary, opened]

## D. What it costs

25. **US net interest in the Monthly Treasury Statement: $970.4 bn in FY2025, $1,051.8 bn in FY2026 (to 30 Sep 2026)**, against $659.2 bn in FY2023 and $881.7 bn in FY2024. FiscalData MTS table 9. https://fiscaldata.treasury.gov/datasets/monthly-treasury-statement/ [primary, opened]
26. **Net interest has exceeded national defence since FY2024**: FY2024 $881.7 bn vs $874.0 bn (a $7.7 bn margin); FY2025 $970.4 bn vs $916.6 bn; FY2026 $1,051.8 bn vs $965.2 bn (interest = 8.2 percent of total outlays $12,799 bn, computed).
